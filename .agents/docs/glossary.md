# 📖 Wada Colors & Japanese Aesthetics Glossary

Reference glossary for classical Japanese color terms, Wada theory, and architectural concepts.

---

## 1. Classical Japanese Color Terminology

- **Haishoku Sōkan (配色の総覧)**: The comprehensive dictionary of color combinations published by Sanzo Wada in 1933.
- **Sumi (墨)**: Traditional lampblack ink; used as the deep carbon neutral for typography and dark surfaces (`#111314`).
- **Washi (和紙)**: Traditional mulberry paper; used as the warm, ivory neutral background bridge (`#fcfbf9`).
- **Shitate (仕立て)**: Tailoring and garment construction; structuring layers for aesthetic balance.
- **Kasane (襲・重ね)**: Historical Heian-era layered color harmony, traditionally used in multi-layered kimonos and adapted here for modern fashion ensembles.
- **Iki (粋)**: Understated, effortless elegance and sophistication.
- **Wabi-Sabi (侘寂)**: Appreciation of simplicity, natural textures, and unpretentious beauty.
- **Ma (間)**: Negative space or spatial pause; in UI design, clean whitespace preventing color clash.

---

## 2. Technical & Mathematical Terms

- **CIELAB ($L^*a^*b^*$)**: Perceptually uniform color space where $L^*$ is lightness, $a^*$ is green-to-red, and $b^*$ is blue-to-yellow.
- **Delta-E ($\Delta E^*$)**: Metric for the perceived distance between two colors in CIELAB space.
- **Relative Luminance**: Perceived brightness of a color, normalized from 0 (black) to 1 (white), used for WCAG contrast calculation.
- **WCAG AA / AAA**: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines contrast ratios (4.5:1 / 7:1 for text).
