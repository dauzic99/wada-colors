# 🏛️ Spatial & Interior Architecture Rules (7-Plane Allocation)

> Architectural guideline for translating Wada Sanzo 1930s Japanese color harmonies into physical spaces, retail boutiques, cafes, and residential interiors.

---

## 1. The 7-Plane Spatial Allocation Hierarchy

When applying Wada palettes to architectural environments, colors must be distributed across 7 spatial planes to prevent color overwhelm and ensure serene, balanced harmony:

| Plane | Architectural Element | Recommended Allocation | Role in Wada Palette |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Ceilings & Soffits** | Washi lime plaster, off-white acoustic plaster, raw cedar slats | High-reflectance neutral bridge (`#fcfbf9` or light wood) |
| **02** | **Primary Focal Wall** | Accent wall, headboard panel, reception backdrop, fireplace hearth | Dominant or atmospheric Wada color (`c1` or `c2`) |
| **03** | **Secondary Walls** | Perpendicular boundary walls, corridor partitions | Muted washi plaster, warm gray, or raw microcement |
| **04** | **Flooring & Rugs** | Honed terrazzo, wide-plank oak, tatami-edged wool rugs, tatami mats | Grounding foundation tone (`c1`, dark neutral, or natural oak) |
| **05** | **Fixed Millwork & Joinery**| Kitchen cabinetry, built-in shelving, bar counters, door frames | Matte lacquer in `c1`/`c2` or blackened steel / hinoki timber |
| **06** | **Focal Furniture & Upholstery** | Curved modular sofa, dining banquette, accent lounge armchairs | Tactile textile upholstery in `c2` or `c3` (bouclé, linen, velvet) |
| **07** | **Sculptural Decor & Lighting** | Ceramic vessels, pendant lamps, lacquer coffee table, metal accents | High-chroma Wada accent (`c3` or `c4`) |

---

## 2. Lighting & Color Temperature Guidelines

- **Residential & Lounge Hospitality**:
  - Color Temperature: **2700K Warm White**.
  - Lighting Type: Hidden cove indirect illumination, diffused paper lanterns (Akari style), low-level floor wash lights.
  - Atmosphere: Soft shadows, gentle falloff, calm wabi-sabi ambiance.
- **Retail, Gallery & Contemporary Workspace**:
  - Color Temperature: **3000K – 3500K Neutral Warm**.
  - Lighting Type: Recessed architectural directional spotlights, linear wash on textured focal walls.
  - CRI (Color Rendering Index): **CRI $\ge 95$** to faithfully reveal authentic Wada pigments without color distortion.

---

## 3. Materiality & Tactile Synergy

Authentic Japanese color harmonies thrive when paired with organic, tactile materials:

- **Earthy Palettes** (e.g. #14, #32, #161):
  - *Pair with*: Unlacquered brass, rammed earth, reclaimed teak, rough stoneware ceramics, raw linen.
- **Cool Botanical & Ocean Palettes** (e.g. #121, #281):
  - *Pair with*: Silver-gray honed limestone, pale hinoki cypress, glazed celadon tiles, fluted frosted glass.
- **Dramatic Noir & Jewel Palettes** (e.g. #130, #241, #330):
  - *Pair with*: Blackened hot-rolled steel, smoked oak, honed dark basalt, velvet upholstery, hand-applied gold leaf accents.
