## 4. Spatial Harmonization Rules

- **Neutral Envelope Rule**: Maintain walls or flooring in a calming monochrome wash (`#fcfbf9` washi plaster or `#1a1e24` dark lime plaster) to allow the authentic Wada furniture and textile colors to anchor the eye.
- **Lighting Temperature**: 2700K warm diffuse architectural lighting enhances the vintage Showa warmth of Wada pigments.
